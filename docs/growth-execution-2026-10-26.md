October 26, 2026. Planning assumptions are not revenue, sales forecasts or guarantees.

## Positioning and offers
Lead with custom creator systems and specific compatible deliverables. Direct high-intent visitors to the matching service page, a fictional studio demo labeled as such, transparent starting scope and Contact Us Now. Do not present assembled previews as production-ready files. Feature the $299 static Starter for small rebrands, the shared-master $249 alert family for focused upgrades, cinematic kits for rebrand/debut work, and $499/month defined clip editing only when capacity is confirmed.

Week 4: compare actual form inquiries, qualified leads, accepted quotes, verified deposits, collected revenue, production hours, revisions and contribution. Improve the service page or qualification step that shows the largest evidenced loss. Offer recurring clip editing to suitable customers only after delivery capacity and written renewal/cancellation terms are clear.

## Ethical links and community work
Create useful setup checklists and short demonstrations relevant to streamer and VTuber communities. Follow community promotion rules. Propose creator collaborations, software tutorials and editorial pitches for owner review. Seek links because the work is useful; do not buy links, fabricate client endorsements, post disguised testimonials or claim guaranteed rankings. No publication or external pitch is sent by this document.

## Revenue math
A hypothetical mix of 4 Cinematic Reign projects at $1,299 is $5,196 in quoted project value; 8 Ascension projects at $649 is $5,192; 11 editing retainers at $499 is $5,489 monthly quoted value. These are arithmetic scenarios, not bookings, collected revenue, profit or capacity promises. At a hypothetical 25% qualified-lead-to-sale conversion, four sales need sixteen qualified leads; measure the real rate before using it for staffing or forecasts. A 15% deposit is cash collected toward a project, not the full project total.

## Measurement
Use the existing private inquiry stages and project/quote/invoice records as the source of truth. Deduplicate inquiries and do not count tests, spam or unaccepted quotes as sales. Record cash actually collected separately from invoiced value, outstanding balances and refunds. Keep customer identities out of public dashboards. Reconcile Square with project records before reporting revenue. This release adds no advertising trackers, recurring billing enrollment or automatic marketing.
