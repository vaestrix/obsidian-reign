PROJECT QUESTIONS

A few useful answers.

What if I do not know which assets I need?

Start with your channel, goals, and budget. The creator brief helps us recommend a scope and a visual direction before you commit to production.

Will this work on Twitch, Kick, or YouTube?

Tell us your platform and streaming software in your brief. We confirm the required formats, dimensions, and setup needs when agreeing on the project scope.

How are revisions and delivery agreed?

We confirm scope, revisions, price, and payment milestones before production. Your project details determine the delivery plan.

How do I check on an existing project?

Open Client Login and use the access code provided by the studio. Your project space holds milestones, updates, review links, and feedback.
