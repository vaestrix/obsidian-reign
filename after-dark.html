A few things worth knowing

Good questions.

Do I have to want a gothic look?

No. The collections above show two original concepts. Your brief can be soft, minimal, sporty, playful, anime-inspired, cinematic or completely different. We design around your identity.

Can I order just a banner or watermark?

Yes. Individual assets, a small profile refresh or a complete brand can be quoted. Share your budget and priorities so we can scope a useful starting point.

Can you work with my photos or a faceless identity?

Yes, using material you own or have permission to use. We can scope typography, monograms and original illustration without a face reveal. Any likeness changes require your approval.

What will it cost, and when will it be ready?

We confirm the price, asset count, delivery date and revision allowance in writing before payment. Motion, custom illustration and websites need different amounts of production time. Share your launch date in the brief.

Will I get editable files and commercial usage rights?

Your proposal lists final formats, whether editable source files are included, and the agreed usage rights. Fonts, music and other third-party materials may have separate licenses.

Do you manage my account or guarantee subscribers?

These packages are creative design services. They do not include account management or fan messaging, and do not guarantee subscribers, revenue, reach or platform approval.

Can I add alerts, camera surrounds or a full stream theme?

Yes. Tell us your streaming software and layout. We scope dimensions, transparency, audio, installation notes and any required compatibility testing before production.

What content can I commission?

Standard packages are non-suggestive by default. The public showcase may include mildly suggestive, non-explicit glamour. No age-verification provider is connected yet, so suggestive custom commissions remain on hold until independent age verification and studio approval are available. Do not send identity documents through the brief or by email.

Are the previews client results?

No. They are original studio concepts demonstrating a visual direction, not client endorsements, conversion results or completed streaming exports.
